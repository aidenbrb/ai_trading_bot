# SLC v1 preregistration amendment 003 — paper-forward without completed historical qualification

Dated: 2026-08-12.

Parent document SHA-256:
`d453ae039c3d9145e986ff6cf27ce98af7418b7b5ea6da0d2ddb728e801c3e9d`.

This amendment changes no signal, entry, exit, portfolio, cost, or
Section 6 qualification-metric rule. It changes only the sequencing
requirement in Section 6 that historical qualification must pass before
any paper-forward proposal is permitted.

Section 6 of the preregistration states: "Passing this historical test
does not enable execution. It permits a separate paper-forward proposal
at 0.25% risk for at least 90 calendar days and 30 closed trades. Until
that proposal is separately reviewed, this version is unregistered,
research-only, and structurally unable to submit an order."

This amendment permits proposing and running a paper-forward trial under
Section 6 without first passing Section 6's historical qualification
gates. The full-scope historical backtest begun under this preregistration
is deferred, not abandoned: its cache and results directory remain
untouched and the run remains resumable at any time.

This does not weaken any other part of Section 6. Real order submission —
including paper order submission — still requires a separate,
human-authored, hash-recorded proposal document, separately reviewed,
before this strategy is registered as anything other than unregistered
and research-only. That proposal must commit any paper-forward trial to
run for at least 90 calendar days and 30 closed trades before its results
are used to evaluate any further step.
